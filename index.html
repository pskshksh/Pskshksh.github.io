// the story

I fell for engineering because it never lets you stop learning — and mastering new things is the part I love most.

curiosity-driven end-to-end ownership systems thinking GenAI

It started with a fascination for how large systems hold together under pressure. From the classes préparatoires in Le Havre to an engineering degree in Tours, I kept chasing the same question: how do you make software fast, resilient, and simple to operate at scale?

Seven years later I've built and operated backend platforms end to end — designing Golang microservices on Kubernetes, architecting serverless AWS systems, and re-architecting a real-time event pipeline to sustain 50,000 events per second. I care about the whole path: architecture, code, delivery, and the on-call reality of production.

Then Generative AI arrived, and my instinct to learn kicked in again. I now ship LLM-powered features in production — Claude integrations, MCP servers, and RAG systems — with the same engineering rigor: structured outputs, guardrails, evaluation, and cost tracking.

Outside the terminal you'll find me on a football pitch or a padel court, or somewhere new — I've explored 37 countries across Europe, Africa and Asia. Same drive, different maps.
